Bizverse: Empire Builder Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Game data

Bizverse saves your player profile, settings and simulated game progress on your device. The game does not require a Bizverse account. You can export or import a save file. Android may include app data in device backup depending on your settings.

Advertising

The Android app uses Google AdMob to show banner and rewarded ads. Google may process IP address, app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and device identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit. Where available, you can manage ad privacy choices in the app profile and reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings.

Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Deleting local data

Clear app storage in Android settings to remove the local game save. Manage exported copies and Android backups separately.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to chronoitofficial@gmail.com.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
before you start.

Is the money real?

No. All currency, stocks, crypto, dividends, assets and rewards exist only in the game. They have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn. Bizverse does not provide investment advice or live exchange data.

Do I have to watch ads to play?

No. You can tap to earn and play the core simulation without rewarded ads. Natural days progress every five minutes while playing. Rewarded ads optionally skip one day or activate ₹10 per tap for 60 seconds. Banner ads may appear during play.

Can I play offline?

The core simulation runs on your device. Ads need an internet connection and may not always be available. Progress is stored locally, and you can export a save from Profile.

Where can I download Bizverse?

The Google Play release is being prepared. Availability will depend on the published listing in your region. This website does not offer an APK download.

How can I get help or manage my data?

Visit our support page for contact and save guidance. Read our Privacy Policy for local storage, advertising and deletion information.

Support for Bizverse: Empire Builder on Android.

Contact support

Email chronoitofficial@gmail.com.

For a game issue, include your device model, Android version, game version and the steps that caused the problem. A screenshot can help. Do not send passwords or advertising identifiers.

Back up your progress

Open Profile in the game and export your save. Keep the exported file somewhere safe. You can import a compatible save from Profile. We do not provide an account-based cloud recovery service.

Remove local game data

On Android, open Settings → Apps → Bizverse → Storage and clear the app’s storage. Menu names vary by device. This removes the local save. Manage exported files and Android backups separately. There is no Bizverse account to delete.

Advertising and privacy

Where available, open ad privacy options from Profile. Rewarded ads require internet access and may not always be available. If an ad closes before a reward is confirmed, the reward is not granted. You can continue core gameplay without completing a rewarded ad.

Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for more information.

About this website

This static website has no login, contact form, advertising scripts, analytics scripts or embedded third-party media. Contact links open your email application. Your hosting provider may process standard web request logs under its own policies.
